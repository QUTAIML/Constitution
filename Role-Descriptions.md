# Expectations of AIML Executives

All AIML Executives are expected to:

- Regularly attend AIML events and Executive Team meetings  
- Take initiative and proactive ownership for their area of the club  
- Act in a professional and ethical manner, and positively represent the club  
- Commit to the expected weekly time commitment for their specified role  

## About Executive Roles

There are several roles that you can volunteer for in 2025. Each role includes:

- A specific set of responsibilities  
- An estimated time commitment  

> [!NOTE]
> These responsibilities and commitments are not definitive and **will increase** in the lead-up to major events.

## Eligibility

All members nominating for an Executive Role at the **AGM** must be planning to **continue their studies at QUT in the following year**  
(See **Section 5** of the Constitution).

# Admin Team
Manages the club’s internal operations, governance, finances, and compliance with university requirements.

## President
> [!NOTE]
> Requires previous AIML Executive experience 1yr, 10-12 hours per week, in-person event attendance required

The President leads the club's overall direction and serves as its main ambassador, guiding the Executive Team while representing AIML to the university and beyond.

Responsibilities:
- Provide strategic leadership and oversee the Society's operations and direction.
- Chair executive meetings and represent the Society with students, faculty, and industry.
- Support executives, monitor progress, and guide major initiatives.
- Ensure governance, financial oversight, and constitutional compliance.

## Vice President
> [!NOTE]
> Requires previous AIML Executive experience 1yr, 6-8 hours per week, in-person event attendance required

The Vice-President is the understudy to the President and will fill in for the President if the President is away. The Vice-President is also the strategic planning director and works closely with the President to ensure club operations are in line with our objectives.

Responsibilities:
- Support the President and assist with strategic planning and operations.
- Coordinate collaboration between teams and oversee cross-team initiatives.
- Step into the President's responsibilities when required.
- Help drive organisational growth and continuous improvement.

## Secretary
> [!NOTE]
> Recommended previous club experience 1 semester, 2-4 hours per week

The Secretary is the backbone of the club, playing a critical administrative role that ensures smooth operation and keeps everything organised. Managing meetings and club correspondence.

Responsibilities:
- Manage meeting agendas, minutes, records, and official documentation.
- Maintain Society records and ensure administrative compliance.
- Coordinate executive communications and organisational processes.
- Support governance and constitutional requirements.

## Treasurer
> [!NOTE]
> Recommended previous club experience 1 semester, 3-6 hours per week

The Treasurer oversees the finances of the club. This includes income from funding, sponsors, and events; and club expenditures.

Responsibilities:
- Manage the Society's budget, finances, and reimbursement processes.
- Monitor expenditure and prepare financial reports for the executive.
- Oversee funding applications and financial planning.
- Ensure compliance with Guild financial policies.

# Projects Team
Leads technical initiatives and hands-on projects to help members build skills and experience.

## Projects Director
> [!NOTE]
> Technical expertise required or proven interest in AI & ML, 4-6 hours per week, in-person event attendance expected

The Projects Director works to set the vision and strategic direction for club projects, devising initiatives for members to pursue and guiding the overall roadmap.

Responsibilities:
- Lead the Projects Team and oversee technical projects throughout the semester.
- Coordinate project leads, timelines, and project delivery.
- Foster collaboration, innovation, and practical skill development.
- Guide the long term direction of the Projects Team.
  
## Projects Officer
> [!NOTE]
> Technical expertise recommended, 2-4 hours per week, in-person event attendance expected

The Projects Officer will support the Projects Director in the development of club projects and in the delivery of project nights. They will bring their own unique technical expertise and experience to support members to build their projects.

Responsibilities:
- Lead a technical project from planning through delivery.
- Mentor team members and coordinate weekly project activities.
- Manage progress, documentation, and project outcomes.

# Growth Team
Builds and strengthens the AIML community through social media, design, and communications that engage current and future members.

## Growth Director
> [!NOTE]
> Experience recommended, 4-6 hours weekly

The Growth Director oversees the AIML brand and leads the club's community growth strategy, directing the team to build genuine connection and engagement with members across all platforms and initiatives.

Responsibilities:
- Lead the Society's marketing strategy and brand presence.
- Oversee content creation across social media and promotional channels.
- Coordinate the Growth Team and campaign planning.
- Grow member engagement and strengthen the Society's identity.


## Growth Officer
> [!NOTE]
> Experience not required, 2-4 hours weekly

The Growth Officer supports the club's online presence by creating eye catching graphics, event visuals, and short form videos like Instagram Reels, while also engaging with the community through comments and messages to help AIML grow and stay connected.

Responsibilities:
- Create graphics, posts, and promotional content for Society activities.
- Assist with social media management and marketing campaigns.
- Help promote events and engage the student community.


-----

# Education Team
Plans and delivers educational workshops, tutorials, and learning sessions to help members grow their AIML skills.

## Education Director
> [!NOTE]
> Technical experience or proven interest in AI/ML required, 4-6 hours weekly

The Education Director sets the vision and direction for the club's learning initiatives, ensuring educational content is relevant, accessible, and aligned with member needs.

Responsibilities:
- Lead the Society's educational strategy and technical learning initiatives.
- Coordinate workshops, tutorials, and educational resources.
- Support presenters and maintain high quality learning experiences.
- Expand educational opportunities for members.
  
## Education Officer
> [!NOTE]
> Technical expertise recommended, 2-4 hours per week

The Education Officer assists in developing and delivering learning experiences, creating structured materials, and supporting members in building their AIML knowledge and skills.

Responsibilities:
- Plan and deliver engaging AI and machine learning workshops.
- Develop practical learning materials and technical demonstrations.
- Support members during workshops and answer technical questions.

# Industry Team
Connects the club with industry professionals, companies, and organisations to create meaningful opportunities for members.

## Industry Director
> [!NOTE]
> Experience recommended, 4-6 hours weekly

The Industry Director is responsible for developing industry connections, securing potential speakers, and fostering partnerships with sponsors to support the club’s objectives and strategic vision.

Responsibilities:
- Build relationships with industry partners and external organisations.
- Coordinate sponsorships, networking opportunities, and collaborations.
- Strengthen industry engagement and member career opportunities.
- Support long term partnership development.

## Industry Officer
> [!NOTE]
> Experience not required, 2-4 hours weekly

The Industry Officer supports the Industry Director in building and maintaining the club's external relationships, helping coordinate industry events and communications with partners and stakeholders.

Responsibilities:
- Assist with sponsor communications and partnership activities.
- Support networking events and industry engagement initiatives.
- Maintain professional relationships with external stakeholders.


-----

## Chief Returning Officer

>[!NOTE]
> Requires previous AIML Admin/Director Level experience, 1-2 hours weekly

The Chief Returning Officer is an experienced former high ranking executive who provides independent guidance and mentorship to support the current Executive Team in leading the club effectively.

Responsibilities:
- Offer advice on strategic decisions, governance, and club operations when required.
- Act as an independent and experienced sounding board for the Executive Team.
- Provide input on long-term direction while respecting the autonomy of the current Executive Team
